I am an Associate Professor at HEC Montréal. Before that, I was an Assistant Professor of Economics at Columbia University (2019–2026) and a graduate student at UCSD (2014–2019), where I obtained a Ph.D. in Economics.

My research interests lie at the intersection of macro, labor, and finance. In ongoing work, I quantify the welfare effects of macroeconomic shocks (saving glut, tariffs, AI), with an emphasis on redistribution between workers and asset owners.
